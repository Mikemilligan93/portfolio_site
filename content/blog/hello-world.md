This site is my extended CV. The one-pager tells you who I am and where I have worked; this blog is where the longer thoughts go.

## What to expect

I work as a business analyst and tech writer, so most posts will circle around three things:

- **Requirements** – how to get from a client's wish list to something a team can build.
- **Documentation** – specifications people actually read.
- **Delivery** – what I learned running projects end to end.

## How often

No schedule. I would rather publish a useful post now and then than a filler post every week.
